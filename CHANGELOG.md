# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Charon, an accessibility service that credits time in patrons' apps and sites and guards the streams of Lethe.
- Charon's gate over feeds: the "why" question, a breathing pause, 5 / 15 / 30 minutes, the meter, Hermes' staff passes.
- Tariffs: Nyx's hour, early morning, a rising fare; debt to Hades with interest.
- Pantheon with five built-in patrons and custom ones (name, symbol, glaze, rate, daily cap, sound with the screen off, apps, sites).
- Lethe with six built-in streams and custom ones; "only the endless feed" for Instagram, YouTube, VK, Facebook and Snapchat.
- Rites by a face-down timer, the daily ostrakon and a four-week frieze.
- English and Russian, black-figure and red-figure themes.
